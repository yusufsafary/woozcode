Guide

How WoozCode
works

WoozCode borrows two ideas and keeps them simple. Minting works like the agentic pass collections before it: agents only, gated by Binance Agent ID. On top of that sits a referral quota, borrowed from share style launches: your minting room grows when wallets you invited write their first pass.

A

Writing a pass

A pass is minted by an agent, never by a browser. There is no connect button on this site and no mint button either. Your agent reads the instructions at wooz-skill.md, checks that your wallet holds a Binance Agent ID, and calls the mint function on BNB Chain.

  • Supply 5,555. Pass numbers run from 1 to 5,555 and are issued in order.
  • One per call. Each mint call writes exactly one pass.
  • Platform fee. A small fee in BNB applies per mint. It appears only on your agent wallet confirmation screen, never on this site.
  • Quota applies. A wallet can never hold more minted passes than its current quota, even across many calls.
B

Referral quota

Quota is counted by the contract itself, so the numbers on the home page are the numbers on chain.

  • Start at 5. Every eligible wallet begins with a quota of 5 passes.
  • Invite to grow. Share your wallet address. When a wallet's first ever mint names you as referrer, your invite count rises by 1 and so does your quota.
  • Cap at 20. After 15 successful invites your quota is full at 20. Extra invites are still counted, they just add no more room.
  • No payout. Referral pays in room, not in tokens or BNB. Nobody can farm it with their own wallets: a referral only counts from a different wallet, on its first mint.
C

Claiming $WOOZ

$WOOZ is a fixed supply token of 10,000,000,000. A claim pool of 2,777,500,000 $WOOZ is set aside for pass holders. Every pass claims 500,000 $WOOZ exactly once, through the flow at wooz-claim-skill.md. Claims carry a small anti-Sybil fee in BNB, shown only on the agent wallet confirmation screen. Pool tokens cannot be withdrawn by anyone, so a funded claim stays claimable. Claims open once the pool is funded, and the status on the home page reads straight from the claim contract.

D

Questions

Why is there no mint button?

WoozCode is an agentic collection. Minting is a job for your Binance Agentic Wallet agent, the same way the earlier agentic pass collections worked. This site only reads the chain and explains the rules.

What do I need before minting?

A Binance Agentic Wallet, a Binance Agent ID held by that wallet, and enough BNB for the platform fee and network gas. Your agent checks all three before it calls.

Can a contract or a bot mint for me?

No. The contract rejects calls that do not come straight from a wallet, and it requires a Binance Agent ID. That is deliberate: passes are written by agents acting for people.

What happens if I buy a pass on the market?

You hold the pass and everything attached to it. If its $WOOZ was never claimed, you can claim it. If it was claimed before the sale, that claim is spent. The claim contract tracks this per pass number.

Is $WOOZ traded anywhere?

Not at launch. $WOOZ has no trading tax and no pool at launch. If a pool is ever opened, it will use reserve tokens from the project wallet, and it will be announced on this site first.
