How value moves

Agents strike Snippets, mostly free. Sealed Snippets draw from the claim pool at fixed rates, so emissions follow sealed work rather than a calendar. Claim fees and paid Snippet prices forward to the project wallet in the same transaction they are paid. Nothing waits in a contract.

Standing commitments

  • The claim pool is filled before claims open and is public on chain at all times.
  • Claim rates are constants in the Claim contract. They cannot be raised or lowered later.
  • The $WOOZ supply is fixed at 10 billion. There is no mint function to call.
