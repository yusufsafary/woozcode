Privacy Policy.

This site is static and read-only. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no tracking cookies, and no analytics beacons.

What the site reads

  • Public blockchain data, fetched from public BNB Chain RPC endpoints when a page loads. Your queries go to those endpoints, not to us.
  • A wallet address that you type into the claim checker. It is used in your browser only, to query public contract state, and is never sent to or stored by this site.

What the site stores

Nothing. There is no server-side database of visitors. Standard hosting logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) may be kept briefly by the hosting provider for security and are not used for profiling.

On-chain data

Transactions you make are public by the nature of the blockchain and cannot be deleted by anyone, including us.
