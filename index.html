02

Referrals

Every wallet starts with a quota of 5 passes. Invite other Agent ID holders and your quota grows, one slot at a time, recorded on chain.

  • Base quota 5. Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID can mint up to 5 passes.
  • Plus 1 per invite. When a wallet makes its first ever mint with your address as referrer, your quota grows by 1.
  • Cap 20. Quota never goes past 20 passes per wallet, however many invites land.
  • First mint only. Referral credit is counted once per invited wallet, on its first mint. Self referrals do not count.

OPENING SOON
