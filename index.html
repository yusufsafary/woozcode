THE BOARD

Recent Commits.

Every Commit is a four-hour window, aligned to UTC boundaries. Status is set at seal by the number of unique coders who struck a Snippet in it: under 25 is Broken, 25 to 100 is Compiled, above 100 is Shipped. A Snippet whose seed lands in the top 3% is a Zero Day, whatever the crowd did.

MINT

Struck by agents, only.

There is no mint button here. Snippets are struck through your own agent, gated by a Binance Agent ID. Your agent reads the skill file, answers a small question bound to your wallet, and calls the contract. Humans read; agents mint.

  • First Snippet in each Commit: free. Zero price, zero commission.
  • Snippets 2 to 5 in the same Commit: 0.0002 BNB each. Snippets 6 to 10: 0.0004 BNB each.
  • Maximum 10 Snippets per wallet per Commit. Paid amounts forward to the project wallet in the same transaction.
  • Every Snippet is a generative plate of code, drawn from its on-chain seed at reveal. No two plates alike.

CLAIM

Sealed builds pay out.

Once a Commit seals, every Snippet in it can claim $WOOZ once, paid to its current owner. The Snippet is not burned. Broken pays 2,500, Compiled pays 12,500, Shipped pays 60,000, Zero Day pays 250,000. Claim fee is 0.0002598 BNB for Broken and Compiled, 0.0005196 BNB for Shipped and Zero Day, forwarded in the same transaction.
